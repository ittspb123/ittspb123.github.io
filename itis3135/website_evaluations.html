Website Evaluations

These evaluations compare Contrast, Repetition, Alignment, and Proximity: the four CRAP design principles.

Apple

Example of effective visual design: the homepage organizes its products into distinct promotional sections.

  • Contrast: Large product headings and contrasting backgrounds make the main messages stand out from supporting text.
  • Repetition: Product sections repeat similar headings and calls to action, giving visitors a familiar pattern as they scroll.
  • Alignment: Headings, descriptions, and links follow consistent alignment within their sections, making the page easier to scan.
  • Proximity: Each product's name, description, and related links stay together, so visitors can tell which actions belong to that product.

Arngren

Example of ineffective visual organization: the homepage places many product pictures, prices, and links close together.

  • Contrast: Many bright colors, images, and text treatments compete for attention, making it difficult to identify the most important content.
  • Repetition: Products appear throughout the page, but inconsistent sizes and text treatments make the repeated listings harder to follow.
  • Alignment: Product images and text do not follow one clear column structure, so the page is harder to scan in a predictable order.
  • Proximity: Crowded listings leave limited separation between products, which can make it difficult to match a description or price to the right item.
