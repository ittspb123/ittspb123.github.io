Workshop Care Instructions

A simple guide to arranging and caring for Patient Beaver Workshop desk accessories.

Setup | Cables | Cleaning | Maintenance

Set Up Your Desk

Choose a stable, dry surface. Leave enough space to move your keyboard and mouse comfortably.

  1. Place the laptop stand on a flat surface.
  2. Check that your laptop fits securely without blocking its vents.
  3. Place your planner and pen holder within easy reach.

Organize Your Cables

Use the cable organizer to separate charging and accessory cables.

  • Leave enough slack to connect and disconnect each device.
  • Keep cables away from drinks and walking areas.
  • Use a label such as LAPTOP to identify similar cables.

Clean the Accessories

Dust with a soft, dry cloth. For a small mark, use a slightly damp cloth and dry the surface immediately.

Do not soak wooden accessories or apply harsh cleaning products.

Check and Store Your Accessories

Check for damage before use. Stop using a stand that rocks, cracks, or no longer supports your device securely.

Store accessories in a dry location away from direct heat.
