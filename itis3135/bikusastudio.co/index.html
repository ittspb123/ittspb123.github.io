Bikusa Studio is a small design and development shop built around one idea: your website should feel like you, not like a template. We plan, design, and build clean, fast, hand coded sites for individuals and small businesses who want something that actually reflects their work instead of looking like everyone else's.

What makes us different

  • Every site is hand coded, not dropped into a page builder
  • We design the palette and layout from scratch for each client
  • Fast page loads, because bloated sites lose visitors
  • Accessible by default: readable contrast, keyboard-friendly navigation
  • Mobile first layouts that hold up on any screen size
  • Plain language project updates, no jargon walls
  • Small enough to actually know your project, not just your ticket number

What we can do for you

Whether you need a first website from scratch, a redesign of something that's starting to feel dated, or ongoing updates as your business grows, we can scope it, build it, and hand it off with clear documentation so you're never locked out of your own site.
